# Arm / Leg Split — Workout Routine

This is a 4-day split rotated through the week: two leg days and two arm/upper days, alternated.

**Conventions used in every routine below:**
- Every exercise is **3 sets of N reps** unless otherwise stated.
- The number in `(parentheses)` at the start of an exercise line is the **target rep count per set**.
- A weight after the exercise name is the **working weight** (the last set, when not noted otherwise). Units are either `lb` (pounds) or `kg` (kilograms).
- A list in parentheses at the end of a line (e.g. `(45 55 65)`) gives the **per-set weight progression** across the 3 sets.
- A list prefixed with `warmup` (e.g. `(warmup 45 70)`) means those listed weights are warmup sets done before the working set.
- Notes in `[brackets]` are technique cues or alternates.
- "BW" / no weight = bodyweight.
