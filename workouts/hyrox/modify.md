# **Gym Workouts — Hyrox Modified**

Your existing Leg 1/Leg 2/Arm routines modified with Hyrox-specific substitutions. Changes from your original routines are marked with ⭐.

## **Tuesday AM — Leg 1 (Strength Focus)**

Heavier loads, lower reps. Builds the raw strength for sled push/pull and sandbag lunges.

| \# | Exercise | Sets×Reps | Weight | Hyrox Transfer / Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **1** | **Squat (barbell)** | 3×6 | 65 lb (45,55,65) | Sled push quad drive. Keep your progression. |
| **2** | **Romanian deadlift** | 3×10 | 45 lb (35,45,45) | Sled pull posterior chain. Keep as-is. |
| **3** | **Hip thrusts** | 3×12 | 25 lb | Sled push hip extension. Keep as-is. |
| **4** | **⭐ Farmer carry** | 3×200m | 24kg/hand KBs | NEW: Replaces leg extensions. Station 6 practice. Build to unbroken 200m. |
| **5** | **Lying leg curl** | 3×12 | 7.5 kg | Hamstring protection. Keep as-is. |
| **6** | **⭐ Nordic hamstring curls** | 2×8 | Bodyweight | NEW: Replaces hip machine. Protects hamstrings and calves from cramping. |
| **7** | **Crunch \+ ⭐ Dead hang** | 3×12 \+ 3×30s | 83 lb / BW | MODIFIED: Add dead hangs after crunches. Builds grip for sled pull rope and farmer carry. |

## **Friday AM — Leg 2 (Station Simulation)**

Moderate loads, higher reps, station-specific movements. Every exercise maps to a Hyrox station. Running PM on these tired legs \= compromised running practice.

| \# | Exercise | Sets×Reps | Weight | Hyrox Transfer / Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **1** | **Deadlift** | 3×8 | 90 lb (WU 45,70) | Sled pull \+ posterior chain. Keep your progression. |
| **2** | **⭐ DB walking lunges (on shoulders)** | 3×50m | 25 lb DBs | MODIFIED: Hold DBs on shoulders (not at sides) to mimic 20kg sandbag position for Station 7\. Build to 100m unbroken. |
| **3** | **⭐ Wall balls** | 3×30 reps | 6kg ball to 3m | NEW: Station 8 practice. Build to unbroken 30+. Focus on squat depth and breathing rhythm. |
| **4** | **⭐ Goblet squat** | 3×15 | Moderate KB | NEW: Replaces single-leg extension. High-rep quad endurance for sled push and wall balls. |
| **5** | **⭐ Plate pinch hold** | 3×30s | 2×10kg plates | NEW: Replaces single-leg curl. Grip endurance for farmer carry and sled pull rope. |
| **6** | **⭐ Dead hang** | 3×max | Bodyweight | NEW: Replaces seated hip. More grip work. Track your max hold time. |
| **7** | **Calf raise** | 3×12 | Bodyweight | Keep as-is. Calf maintenance. |
